---
name: Campaign guardrails
description: Product boundaries for dedicated event campaigns and unverified event content.
---

New event campaigns must remain separate from the main product homepage and must not consume eligibility for an older event offer. The Paris offer includes a creator pass and a friend pass as equally important parts, not an optional referral.

**Why:** The campaign request explicitly preserves the homepage and VivaTech/Cannes behavior and identifies the friend pass as core to the offer.

**How to apply:** Keep campaign styling and copy route-scoped. Check cross-campaign email eligibility when sharing voucher storage. Do not invent event dates, venues, or competition rules; obtain confirmed details before publishing those claims.

Paris campaign visuals and copy must follow the supplied reference artwork, not a new editorial concept. Use futuristic sans-serif typography throughout, the original Paris Creator Week logo, and a dark chromatic blue/violet/magenta identity rather than a dominant flat pink treatment. Friend passes are separate Creator Passes, not co-piloting or participation in the first creator's videos.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected invented slogans, co-pilot/shared-video framing, static abstract diagrams, weak voucher rectangles, and unnecessary explanatory copy. The campaign's energetic video-commerce identity is intentional.

**How to apply:** Keep copy concise and sourced from the references or explicit instructions. Use dimensional, interactive vouchers and motion with reduced-motion support. Keep share controls icon-only with accessible labels, forms minimally titled with accessible field labels, and the cash-prize section prominent with galaxy-only decoration. Never invent a euro valuation for the passes.

The passes are MTRLZD Creator Passes, not Paris Creator Week information or event-admission passes. Keep MTRLZD first in the header. The Paris Creator Week logo must be the original full-color supplied image, not an extracted/recolored wordmark. The campaign must use a galaxy background, not architectural imagery, and exactly three content sections: video-led hero, exclusive competition, signup with vouchers. Reuse the homepage product-carousel presentation and rounded section conventions rather than redesigning them.

**Why:** The user repeated the background requirement and corrected pass branding, the altered logo, unnecessary sections, and the bank-card-like vouchers. The campaign remains part of MTRLZD, not a separately branded event site.

**How to apply:** Treat later explicit copy instructions as overriding older reference art. Use ticket-shaped vouchers, minimal copy, and a centered home-linked MTRLZD logo instead of a full footer. Competition entry should lead directly to signup, and prize motion should repeat while visible, respecting reduced motion.
